AI research lead in Paris. I take speech and multimodal models from large-scale training to products people use.

### What I work on

- **[BidirLM](https://huggingface.co/BidirLM)** (COLM 2026): an open-source recipe that turns causal LLMs into text, image and audio encoders. 5 models released, 45k+ downloads. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02045)
- **[DocuFind](https://github.com/Theodb/DocuFind)**: a free, open-source macOS app that searches your documents, photos and recordings by meaning, fully on-device with BidirLM-Omni.
- At **[Diabolocom](https://www.diabolocom.com)**, I lead applied research for enterprise clients:
  - **Speech recognition:** post-training with SFT and reinforcement learning (WER reward).
  - **Speech-to-speech:** full-duplex speech-to-speech models for real-time voice agents.
  - **LLM judges and PII detection** for customer calls.
